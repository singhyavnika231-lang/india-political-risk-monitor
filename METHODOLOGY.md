# Methodology

## Purpose

The India Political Risk Monitor is an experimental, event-based research framework designed to track political, geopolitical, security, economic, regulatory and institutional developments affecting India.

It is intended as a transparent research tool rather than an official or definitive measure of political risk.

## Unit of Analysis

The basic unit of analysis is an individual event or development.

Each event is recorded with:

- Date
- Event
- Category
- Sub-category
- Location
- Key actors
- What happened
- Why it matters
- Risk direction
- Impact level
- Source
- Source type

## Risk Categories

Events are classified into five broad categories:

1. Domestic Political Stability
2. Social & Civil Unrest
3. Geopolitical & Security
4. Economic & Regulatory
5. Institutional & Policy

Categories are designed to capture different dimensions of the environment rather than assume that political risk originates from a single source.

## Impact Level

Each event is assigned an impact level from 1 to 5.

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 — Minimal | Limited or localized significance |
| 2 — Low | Limited wider implications |
| 3 — Moderate | Meaningful implications for institutions, markets, society or policy |
| 4 — High | Significant implications across multiple areas or actors |
| 5 — Severe | Potentially extensive or systemic implications |

Impact classification is an analytical judgement based on the information available at the time of entry.

## Risk Direction

Each event is assigned one of three directional classifications:

- **Increasing** — the development indicates a movement toward greater potential political, geopolitical, economic, security or institutional risk within the scope being monitored.
- **Stable** — the development does not materially change the monitored risk environment.
- **Decreasing** — the development indicates a movement toward lower potential risk within the scope being monitored.

These classifications are analytical assessments and should not be interpreted as objective measurements.

## Sources

The project prioritizes established news organizations, official government sources, regulatory bodies, courts and other primary or credible institutional sources.

Where possible, important developments should be cross-checked against more than one source.

The source field records the publication or institution used for the event entry.

## Event Selection

Events are selected when they have a plausible connection to India's:

- political stability
- domestic social environment
- foreign relations or security environment
- economic or regulatory environment
- institutional or policy environment

The dataset is not intended to capture every political or economic development in India.

## Analytical Approach

The project uses an event-based approach rather than attempting to predict future political outcomes.

The dashboard summarizes:

- number of events tracked
- distribution by category
- distribution by risk direction
- distribution by impact level
- changes in the monitored environment over time

As the dataset grows, the project may incorporate time-weighted analysis so that more recent developments have greater analytical relevance than older events.

Any aggregate measure will remain experimental and will be documented transparently.

## Limitations

The project has several important limitations:

- Event selection can introduce researcher bias.
- Impact and direction classifications involve analytical judgement.
- News coverage can vary by source and may not capture every relevant development.
- Categories can overlap.
- A small dataset cannot represent the full complexity of India's political environment.
- The framework is descriptive and exploratory rather than predictive.
- The project does not assign probabilities to future political events or outcomes.

## Research Principle

The objective is not to produce a single definitive number for India's political risk.

The objective is to build a transparent, reproducible and continuously updated dataset that allows political, geopolitical, economic and institutional developments to be examined systematically.
